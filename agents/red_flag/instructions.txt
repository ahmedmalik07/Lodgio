### CRITICAL CONFLICTS (AVOID):

- **Noise Hell**: "complete silence needed" + "gaming sessions with friends"

- Budget claims that don't match student status

### CULTURAL MISMATCHES:
- Religious practice conflicts (strict vs liberal)
- Food restrictions that clash
- Social activity level extremes

### STUDY CONFLICTS:
- Exam period needs vs music/noise habits
- Group study vs solitude preferences
- Academic stress levels (ultra competitive vs laid back)

**CRITICAL**: Fundamental incompatibility - recommend avoiding
**HIGH**: Major issues but workable with clear agreements
